# Collection Economics

The updated product model uses collection-specific admin fees, not a universal 97% community share or a token-burn budget. Network gas and Base VRF/CCIP costs are separate and are not assumed to be zero.

## Per-collectible split

| Collection | Type ID | Nominal price | Admin fee | Admin per paid mint | Pool contribution per collectible | Full 500-collectible pool |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Buddy | 3 | $3 | 5% | $0.15 | $2.85 | $1,425 |
| Samurai | 13 | $13 | 3% | $0.39 | $12.61 | $6,305 |
| Noble | 25 | $25 | 2% | $0.50 | $24.50 | $12,250 |

Dollar amounts describe the product model, not a dollar-denominated contract balance or guaranteed allocation. Payments and allocations use native ETH. Read the configured on-chain price before sending a transaction; the production USD-to-ETH conversion policy is not supplied in the contract specification.

## A funded cycle

Five positions are free to the buyer, but all 500 contribute to the pool. FOSNFT funds the five free-position contributions from retained fees/reserves.

| Collection | 495 buyer payments | Paid-position admin fees | Five free contributions | Remaining admin funds | Full cycle pool |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Buddy | $1,485 | $74.25 | $14.25 | $60 | $1,425 |
| Samurai | $6,435 | $193.05 | $63.05 | $130 | $6,305 |
| Noble | $12,375 | $247.50 | $122.50 | $125 | $12,250 |

For each collection, **cycle pool + remaining admin funds = buyer purchase payments**. This assumes nominal product prices, matching cycle contribution settings, and sufficient FOSNFT reserves. Actual accounting uses ETH and integer rounding. The remaining admin amount is before operational and oracle/bridge costs; it is not a profit forecast.

## Independent referral accounting

| Referrer's active plan | Share of a direct referral's payment | Admin share |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| $2 plan | 50% | 50% |
| $5 plan | 60% | 40% |
| $10 plan | 82% | 18% |

The **referrer's own active plan at payment time** determines the split, for both registration and upgrades. The buyer's plan determines the payment amount, not the commission percentage. Without a referrer, 100% goes to the admin. Dollar labels are nominal plan names; exact payment is the configured ETH price.

Referral receipts are separate from mint pools. They do not fund an individual's selection eligibility or alter collectible weights. Any growth-campaign funding must use available admin balances after user liabilities and free-position reserves.

## Comparison limits

The previous economics report mixed illustrative centralized-platform percentages with unsupported FOS-wide return claims. The current tables and chart show the supplied contract arithmetic only. Automated accounting does not eliminate legal requirements, audit costs, chain risks, or delivery delays.
