## Evidence and interpretation

External facts are cited to pinned network registries, Chainlink's own service sources, and the ERC-721 standard, checked for this revision. Product parameters come from the supplied FOS contract specification. Proposed market segments and adoption metrics are analysis; they are not already observed users or revenue.

FOS user transactions belong on Robinhood Chain. Base is the specified randomness-service side. Base usage statistics cannot establish Robinhood or FOS adoption. The research does not publish an unsupported market-size forecast, conversion rate, market share, or universal community-return percentage.

The [market report](/docs/market-insights/market) includes the source list and the distinction between wallet chain IDs, Chainlink selectors, and verified application deployments.
