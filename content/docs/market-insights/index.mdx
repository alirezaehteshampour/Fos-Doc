# Market Research

Current product economics and historical market research serve different purposes.

- [Collection economics](/docs/market-insights/economics): current nominal prices, fee splits, free-position reserves, and full-round arithmetic.
- [2025 market outlook](/docs/market-insights/market-2025): retained historical projections and Base-market context, not Robinhood adoption data.
- [Giveaway trust analysis](/docs/market-insights/giveaways): retained research illustrations, not measured FOS performance or legal conclusions.

FOS user-facing contracts are now specified on Robinhood Chain. Base remains the randomness-service side. Do not relabel old Base charts as Robinhood statistics or infer current market share from a historical chart.
